ABOUT LOCALCONNECT

LocalConnect is a fast-start community marketplace platform that lets neighbors buy, sell, and offer services to one another.

Sellers can list products with photos, price, category and location. Buyers can browse, filter and message sellers directly — all within a simple, trustworthy digital space designed for local communities.

Trusted Local Trade

Verified profiles and direct in-app messaging build buyer-seller trust.

Promotes Reuse

Encourages second-hand trade and reduces waste in the community.

Hyper-Local Focus

Listings are matched and filtered by neighborhood and distance.

OUR MISSION
Simple & Transparent

Clear listing details, honest pricing, and easy reporting keep the marketplace clean.

Community First

Every feature is built to strengthen connections between neighbors.
